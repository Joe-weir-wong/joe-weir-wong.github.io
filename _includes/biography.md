I am currently an associate professor at the School of Mathematics and Information Engineering at [Longdong University](https://www.ldxy.edu.cn/). I received my Ph.D. degree from the School of Telecommunications Engineering at Xidian University and was advised by [Prof. Wenping Ma](https://web.xidian.edu.cn/mawenping/). My research interests focus on <strong>information security</strong>, <strong>applied cryptography</strong>, and <strong>algebraic coding theory</strong>, with an emphasis on security and privacy-preserving issues for emerging and data-intensive application scenarios.
